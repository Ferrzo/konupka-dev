## Overview

Atos is one of the world's leading IT services companies. As part of their international delivery organisation, I worked on two separate projects for major German enterprise clients — a factory management application for Osram, and a carsharing application for Volkswagen.

## My Role

I worked as a frontend developer contributing to both projects in sequence. On the Osram project, I was part of the team building a cross-platform application using Ionic and Angular. On the Volkswagen project, I contributed to the development of a native iOS carsharing application built with Swift.

## What I Built

**Osram — Factory Management App**
- A cross-platform web and mobile application built with Ionic and Angular, targeting both desktop browsers and mobile devices from a single codebase.
- Screens and features for managing factory processes and production planning.
- UI components and navigation flows designed for use in an industrial environment.

**Volkswagen — Carsharing App**
- A native iOS carsharing application developed in Swift.
- Features supporting vehicle booking, access, and user account management within the carsharing service.

## Challenges & Solutions

Working across two very different technology stacks — Angular/Ionic for Osram and Swift for Volkswagen — within the same period required fast context switching and a willingness to work outside a single comfort zone. Each project also came with the complexity of large enterprise clients with strict requirements and structured delivery processes.

I adapted by focusing on understanding the specific platform conventions for each project and leaning on Agile processes to stay aligned with the team's priorities.

## Results

Both applications were delivered as part of larger enterprise programmes at their respective organisations. The Osram cross-platform app consolidated factory planning tooling into a single accessible interface, while the Volkswagen carsharing app contributed to the company's broader mobility services offering.
