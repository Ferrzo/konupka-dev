## Overview

KARDEF Group is a food supplier focused on frozen fruits and vegetables and cocoa powder products. The site positions the company as a reliable sourcing and logistics partner for manufacturers, food service businesses, and retail buyers.

The goal of the page was to communicate trust quickly, explain the product range clearly, and guide visitors toward contact and quote requests.

## My Role

I worked on the frontend presentation of the marketing site and helped turn a complex B2B offering into a clear, structured web experience. My focus was on information hierarchy, responsive layouts, and conversion-oriented content sections.

## What I Built

- A homepage that highlights the company’s core value proposition and trust message.
- Product and category sections for cocoa powder, frozen fruits, and frozen vegetables.
- Reusable content blocks for flexible supply, direct sourcing, and partner benefits.
- Contact-focused calls to action that encourage quote requests and sales inquiries.
- Responsive page structure that keeps the content readable across desktop and mobile.

## Challenges & Solutions

The main challenge was presenting a broad product catalogue without overwhelming the visitor. I solved that by separating the content into clear sections, keeping the copy benefit-driven, and using a strong visual hierarchy to lead the eye from product value to contact action.

I also kept the messaging consistent across the site so that trust, quality, and delivery reliability were reinforced at every stage of the page.

## Results

The finished site gives KARDEF Group a professional online presence that reflects the quality of its products and logistics capabilities. It makes the company easier to understand for new prospects and supports inbound sales conversations with a clearer path to contact.
